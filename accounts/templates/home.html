Built for files that cannot leak

Every upload, share, and download enforces three cryptographically guaranteed safeguards — so sensitive documents remain 100% confidential.

Zero-Knowledge Storage

Files are transformed into scrambled ciphertext before leaving your machine. Not even VaultShare administrators can read your data.

Granular Access Rules

Configure passcode protection, strict expiration dates, and custom download count thresholds for every link you distribute.

Tamper-Evident Logs

Every access attempt, password validation, and download event is recorded to an immutable, traceable audit trail.

Self-Destruct Links

Links auto-expire immediately once max downloads are met or the timer elapses, scrubbing encrypted payload pointers automatically.

SHA-256 Integrity Checks

Recipients automatically verify the cryptographic hash fingerprint to guarantee files were not altered during transmission.

Client Key Isolation

Decryption keys exist exclusively in browser RAM or key wrappers during active sessions and are never stored in databases.
